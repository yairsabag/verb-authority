# Code of conduct

Verb Authority welcomes precise, good-faith participation from people of all
backgrounds and experience levels.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and open to correction.
- Critique claims, code, tests, and evidence rather than people.
- Preserve contributor attribution and the provenance of external fixtures.
- Minimize sensitive data and follow [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) for security
  reports.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate exposure of private data,
personal attacks, and knowingly misleading security claims are not accepted.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation, or
close discussions that violate this code. To report a conduct concern without
posting its details publicly, open a minimal issue requesting a private contact
channel. Do not include personal or sensitive details in that issue.

This code applies in project spaces and when someone is publicly representing
the project.
